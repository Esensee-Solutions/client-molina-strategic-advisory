# Design brief: Molina Strategic Advisory

Written after the fact, from the Phase 1 site. Confirm the gaps (marked **?**) with Waleska.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Business** | Molina Strategic Advisory (MSA), DBA of Wala Isla Glow LLC. HR, payroll, operations and sales strategy consulting for growing businesses. Founder: Waleska Molina Oyola, MBA-HR, 15+ years. Naranjito, Puerto Rico. |
| **Customers** | Owners of growing businesses with employees but no HR structure, where the owner handles every employee issue. And referral partners (CPAs, attorneys, insurance professionals, business advisors) who pass those businesses along. **?** Device: most likely phone for referrals, desktop for owners. |
| **The site's one job** | Get a consultation booked on the Calendly scheduler (or an email to `waleska@molinastrategicadvisory.com`). |
| **What only they have** | The **MSA Business Growth & Operations Diagnostic**: six areas (People & HR, Payroll, Operations, Sales, Customer Experience, Management) ending in a 30 / 60 / 90 day action plan. |
| **How it should feel** | **?** Waleska's three words. The site reads as: established, structured, calm. Navy and blue from the logo, engraved serif wordmark. |
| **Content** | Hero → the growth-vs-structure problem → four services → the Diagnostic → ways to work with MSA → "a good fit if…" → the founder → referral partners → scheduler. Terms and Privacy pages. |
| **Brand** | Official logo (lockup, mark and share card in `assets/img/`). Palette: navy `#13243F`, `#21406E`, blue `#2C63A8`, `#3A7BC8`, grey `#6E7276`, `#F4F6F9`. Cinzel, Source Serif 4, Inter. No white/knockout logo yet (the footer uses type only). |
| **Likes / dislikes** | **?** |
| **Languages** | English and Spanish, English first. |
| **Avoid** | Published prices of any kind. "ADP Certified" or "ADP Partner". Anything that sounds like legal or tax advice. |
